# DESIGN.md starter

A template for writing your design system down in a form AI design and coding tools can
follow. Copy [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md) into your project, replace the placeholder values,
and point your assistant at it before asking for interface work.

The idea is simple: tools produce generic output when they have nothing specific to
follow. Give them your tokens, your component rules and a checklist, and the output
starts on-system instead of needing to be corrected into it.

The values in the template are placeholders, not a real system.
